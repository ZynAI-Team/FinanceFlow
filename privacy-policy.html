Privacy Policy for FinanceFlow

Last updated: August 15, 2026

FinanceFlow ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal finance management application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name and email address, either directly (email/password sign-up) or via Google Sign-In. This is used to create and secure your account.

Financial information you enter. The App lets you record expenses, income, budgets, goals, and investments (such as gold, shares, EMIs, loans, and other revenue). This data is entered by you and stored so the App can display your history, analytics, and reports.

AI chat messages. If you use the in-app AI assistant, your messages and the assistant's responses are stored so the conversation can continue across sessions, and are processed by our AI provider to generate responses.

Technical information. We may automatically collect basic technical data (such as device type and app version) required for the App to function and to diagnose issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To provide core features: expense/income tracking, budgeting, goal tracking, investment tracking, analytics, reports, and market data
  • To generate AI-powered insights, chat responses, and financial suggestions
  • To improve app reliability and troubleshoot issues

We do not sell your personal or financial information to third parties.

3. How We Store and Share Information

Your account and financial data are stored securely on our backend servers (hosted on AWS) and in a MongoDB database. Authentication is handled by Firebase (Google). Chat messages may be processed by our third-party AI provider (Google Gemini) solely to generate responses to your queries.

We do not share your financial data with advertisers or other third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and financial data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact section below).

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can edit or delete individual financial entries within the App.
  • You can request a copy of your data or full account deletion by contacting us.
  • You can sign out at any time, which ends your active session.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including Firebase Authentication and encrypted network connections (HTTPS), to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

FinanceFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
