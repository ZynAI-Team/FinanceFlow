Privacy Policy for FinanceFlow

Last updated: October 20, 2026

FinanceFlow ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal finance management application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name and email address, either directly (email/password sign-up) or via Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only the private relay address Apple provides. This is used to create and secure your account.

Financial information you enter. The App lets you record expenses, income, budgets, and savings goals. This data is entered by you and stored so the App can display your history, analytics, and reports.

Photos of receipts and documents. If you choose to take a photo or select an image (for example, a receipt) using the camera or photo library, that image is uploaded to our servers. Images attached to an expense or income entry are stored with that entry. Images you send in the AI chat are analysed to answer your question and are not stored; only the text of the conversation is kept. The App only accesses the camera or photo library when you tap to add an image, and only the images you select are uploaded.

AI chat messages. If you use the in-app AI assistant, your messages and the assistant's responses are stored so the conversation can continue across sessions, and are processed by our AI provider to generate responses.

Crash and diagnostic information. If the App crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) collects a crash report containing technical details such as device model, operating system version, app version and the state of the App at the time of the crash. Crash reports are not linked to your name, email or financial data and are used only to find and fix problems.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To provide core features: expense/income tracking, budgeting, goal tracking, analytics, and reports
  • To generate AI-powered insights, chat responses, and general budgeting suggestions
  • To read receipt images you upload and pre-fill transaction details (amount, vendor, category)
  • To improve app reliability and troubleshoot issues

We do not sell your personal or financial information to third parties.

3. How We Store and Share Information

Your account and financial data are stored securely on our backend servers (hosted on AWS) and in a MongoDB database. Images attached to your entries, such as receipts, are stored in Amazon S3 (AWS). Authentication is handled by Firebase (Google) and, if you use it, Sign in with Apple.

To provide AI features, the following may be sent to our third-party AI provider, Google Gemini: your chat messages, a summary of your financial records (so the assistant can answer questions about your own spending), and receipt or document images you upload for scanning or attach in chat. This data is sent solely to generate the requested response and is not used by us for advertising. The App asks for your permission before any data is sent to Google Gemini, and nothing is sent unless you tap Allow. You can withdraw permission at any time in Profile → AI Data Sharing; AI features are then turned off and no further data is sent.

We do not share your financial data with advertisers or other third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and financial data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data, including any uploaded images, is deleted immediately (see Section 5 below). Some limited data may be retained for a short period where required by law, then deleted.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can edit or delete individual financial entries within the App at any time.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data yourself, immediately, from the Profile tab within the App (Profile > Delete Account).
  • You can also request account or data deletion by email if you're unable to access the App. See our Delete Account & Data page for details.
  • You can sign out at any time, which ends your active session.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including Firebase Authentication and encrypted network connections (HTTPS), to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Age Restriction

FinanceFlow is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
