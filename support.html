Frequently asked questions

How do I add an expense or income entry?

Open the relevant tab (Expenses or Income) and tap the add button to log a new entry, including amount, category, and date.

How do I set a budget?

Go to the Budget section and set a limit for a category. FinanceFlow will track your spending against it and alert you as you approach the limit.

How does the AI assistant work?

The in-app AI assistant answers questions about your finances using the transaction, budget, and goal data you've entered. Chat history is saved so you can continue a conversation across sessions.

How do I delete an entry, my data, or my account?

You can delete individual records directly within the app, or permanently delete your account and all associated data yourself from Profile > Delete Account. See our Delete Account & Data page for full instructions, including how to request deletion by email if you can't access the app.

Is my data private?

Yes. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's used.
